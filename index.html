Who am I?

My name is Tiff and I'm an aspiring developer! I have interest and hold experience in data science, robotic process automation, and full-stack development. What's currently taking up most of my time is my senior project: Aftermath. It's a natural disaster recovery web-app designed for surivors who need help with finding resources and figuring out what to do next. You can see more of what I'm up to in the projects page!

Outside of academics and work, I love exploring different creative outlets. You'll probably find me listening to music, playing guitar, or writing. I also really enjoy playing video games and watching shows. And when I'm not hunkered down in my room, I like to be one with nature; kayaking, hiking, and rock climbing are some of the pathways to my heart.
