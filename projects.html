Aftermath is a natural disaster recovery web app designed for survivors who need help finding resources and figuring out what to do next. It is actively being built as my senior project with Ark Labs, a team of six that includes five UXID majors focused on front-end development and design. I help lead back-end development alongside one of our teammates, who focuses on database design.

  • Lead backend architecture for web-based application, translating UX team user flows into data models
  • Design MongoDB schemas and build the Express/Node.js API layer for a React/TypeScript frontend deployed on Vercel
  • Integrate government and open-data APIs (NWS, FEMA) to surface location-based disaster alerts and nearby resources
